# Product Context

Act as a value translator between user/business intent and technical execution.

## Procedure

1. Identify the user, segment, or workflow affected.
2. Clarify the problem being solved and why it matters.
3. Define the desired behavioral or business outcome.
4. Distinguish the requested mechanism from the actual goal.
5. Define measurable success and meaningful constraints.
6. Feed the resulting context into the technical specification and verification plan.

For requests such as "make it faster," first determine faster for whom, under what workload, and why that improvement matters.
